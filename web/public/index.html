Just looking around?

Try a small fictional history first. Demo data never becomes part of your journal.

Download the example CSV
What should the export contain?

Each row is a separate event. Required: timestamp (ts) and action. Actions include bet, win, loss, deposit, withdraw, login, logout and near_miss.

Use stake on bets and payout on wins. Payout means total returned, including any returned stake. Provide userId and sessionId if available. Timestamps should include a timezone. Missing sessions are inferred after a 30-minute gap.

Missing money is reported as unknown. Files with omitted rows give partial results. Do not combine different currencies.

Your data, your choice

Guest entries and plans stay in this browser. An optional account lets you enable cloud sync for your journal and plan. Signing in does not automatically upload guest notes. Raw gambling-history files are analysed locally and are never included in sync. Device copies and downloaded backups are not encrypted by this app. Use a private device.

Private browsing, clearing browser data, moving to another address, or changing devices can remove access to saved entries. Without “Remember”, entries last only until refresh or closing this page.

Exports contain personal notes in plain text. Keep them somewhere private. Erasing here does not delete downloaded backups.

Restoring replaces the current journal and plan after confirmation. It does not enable device storage automatically.

Optional accounts and cloud storage

Account sync sends your journal and plan to our Cloudflare-hosted service over an encrypted connection. This is not end-to-end encryption: the service can access synced records. Google sign-in identifies your account; this app does not receive your Google password. Do not use a shared browser profile for private notes.

Your account page shows whether sync is enabled, pending or unavailable. Erasing device data here does not delete cloud records. Use account deletion on the account page to delete the application account and its synced journal. Downloaded backups are outside that deletion and must be removed separately. Hosting providers may retain operational backups under their retention policies; deletion is not a promise of immediate erasure from every backup.

This app is not monitored for emergencies. No one reviews your entries to dispatch help. Support links lead to independent services with their own terms and privacy practices.

What the numbers mean

The experimental Hook Score is a weighted description of recorded patterns. Its weights and thresholds have not been clinically validated. It cannot measure recovery, diagnose addiction, or prove targeting. A low score does not mean no harm.

Historical return is money returned divided by money staked in the provided records. It is not a forecast. We do not calculate your chance of winning money back.

Sources checked 23 September 2026: NHS, GamCare, Gambling Commission: RTP.

CLEAR ANSWERS / NO GUESSWORK

A few useful answers.

How your space works, what is saved, and where to find help.

Do I need an account?

No. You can use check-ins, your journal, the pause and support pages as a guest. An account is optional and cloud sync requires a separate choice.

Will my writing still be here when I return?

By default, entries and plans last only in the current page. Refreshing or closing loses them. Enable “Remember my journal and plan on this browser” on the check-in page to keep device copies and unfinished drafts. Private browsing or clearing site data can remove those copies. Export a backup if you want an additional copy.

What happens when I sign in?

Signing in does not automatically upload guest notes. Your account page explains sync and offers an explicit import. Only enable sync when you want journal entries and your shared plan stored by the service. Check the visible sync status before closing; offline changes may still be waiting.

Is this end-to-end encrypted?

No. Cloud connections use encrypted transport, but the service can access synced records. Device journal copies and exported backups are not encrypted by this app. Keep personal notes on a private browser profile and store backups carefully.

What do my chosen topics mean?

They are choices about what you want to reflect on, not labels applied to you. You can choose more than one and change your current focus. A check-in belongs to the focus selected when it is saved. Your next-step plan is shared across topics.

Does the gambling-history tool upload my file?

No. It analyses supported event exports in your browser. Raw files are not included in account sync. This tool describes supplied gambling records; it does not diagnose addiction or predict future wins.

Why are Echoes only available for gambling?

Echo matching was designed around gambling check-ins. We do not apply that rule to other topics or treat abstinence as a universal success measure. Your other entries are still available in the journal.

Can this app provide treatment or emergency help?

No. This is a reflection and signposting tool, not treatment or a monitored service. No one watches entries to dispatch help. In immediate danger in the UK, call 999. Support links are UK-focused; outside the UK, use local services and your local emergency number.

If you may be dependent on alcohol, get medical advice before stopping or sharply reducing. For drugs or medication concerns, speak with a qualified clinician or relevant service before changing dependent use, and your prescriber before changing prescribed medication.

How do I delete my information?

Support & privacy lets you erase the device copy. This does not delete cloud records. Your account page provides application-account deletion. Downloaded backups must be deleted separately. Read the privacy information for the limits of deletion and provider backups.

How can I report a technical problem?

Use the public project issue tracker linked below. Describe the page, device/browser and steps that caused the problem. Reports are public: never include journal entries, account tokens, gambling exports or other private records. This is technical reporting, not a support or crisis inbox.
